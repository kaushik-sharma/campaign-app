# Campaigns: demo video script

About 4 minutes 50 seconds. Every scene lists what to **do** on screen and what to **say** while doing it. Read the "Say" lines at a relaxed pace (about 140 words a minute); the timings already include the waits for animations and loading.

## Before you record (2 minutes)

1. **Fresh sample data.** Profile → long-press the version number at the bottom → Developer controls → **Reset sample data**.
2. **No surprise errors.** In Developer controls set **Request failure rate: Off**, **Latency: Fast**, **Review decision: Approve**, and keep **Advance on timers** on.
3. **Signed out.** Profile → sign out, so the backing scene runs as a guest.
4. **Sample account exists** in Firebase: `demo@campaigns.app` / `demo1234`.
5. **Phone set-up.** Light mode, Do Not Disturb on, volume up for haptics if your recorder captures them, and close other apps.
6. **Start on Home**, scrolled to the top.

## 1. Home (0:00 – 0:20)

**Do:** Start on Home. Hold for a beat, then scroll slowly down past "Ending soon" and "Now promoting", and scroll back to the top.

**Say:**
> This is Campaigns. It helps important messages reach people beyond their creator's own followers. A creator publishes a message with its sources, our team reviews it, and backers pool money that pays for ads to carry it further. Home shows a featured campaign, the ones closing soon, and the ones already running ads.

## 2. Explore (0:20 – 0:40)

**Do:** Tap the **Explore** tab. Tap the **Health** chip, then **All**. Tap **Trending** and choose **Closest to goal**.

**Say:**
> In Explore you can search by keyword, creator or place, filter across ten categories, and sort. Every card shows the same three things: money raised, time left, and roughly how many people the ads will reach.

## 3. The campaign page (0:40 – 1:10)

**Do:** Tap the **Home** tab, then tap the featured card, **Keep the Riverside Wetlands Wild**. Let the cover glide into place. Tap the small **Est.** tag next to the reach figure, glance at the sheet, and close it. Scroll slowly through the story to **Where your money goes**.

**Say:**
> A campaign page makes its case with evidence. Up top: what's raised, the goal, the backers and the days left. Reach is always a range, labeled as an estimate, and the assumptions are one tap away. The story links its statistics to their sources, and before anyone pays, it shows where every dollar goes: ninety percent buys ads, ten percent covers review, setup and reporting.

## 4. Back a campaign as a guest (1:10 – 1:55)

**Do:** Scroll back up and tap **Back this campaign**.
- Tap **$50** and pause so the reach estimate visibly updates. Tap **Continue**.
- On the review step, let the fee breakdown sit on screen. Tap **Continue to payment**.
- Type a short email, for example `me@test.com`. Leave **Test payment result** on **Approve**. Tap **Pay $51.75**.
- Let the confetti finish, then tap **Done**.

**Say:**
> Backing takes under a minute, and you don't need an account. As I choose fifty dollars, the reach updates live. The review step is completely upfront: card processing is added on top, so the full fifty counts toward the goal, and if a campaign raises less than two hundred fifty dollars, everyone is refunded. This prototype uses a test card, so no real money moves. Approve... and my contribution moves the campaign's progress, with a receipt sent by email.

## 5. Follow and sign in (1:55 – 2:20)

**Do:** Back on the campaign page, tap the **bell** button next to **Back this campaign**. The sign-in screen opens. Tap **Fill in sample account**, then **Sign in**. The screen closes and the bell is now on.

**Say:**
> Following a campaign needs an account, so the app asks me to sign in and tells me why. Accounts are real. I'll use our sample creator account. And notice: once I'm signed in, the follow I started just completes. No second tap.

## 6. Create a campaign by voice (2:20 – 3:15)

**Do:** Tap the **Create** tab, then **Create with your voice**.
- For each of the four questions: tap the **microphone**, wait for the answer to finish typing, then tap **Next**.
- On the fourth question, tap **Draft my campaign** and let the drafting animation run. The editor opens on step 1.

**Say** (spread across the four questions):
> Creating a campaign is guided. Instead of facing a blank form, I can answer four questions out loud: what people should know... why it matters now... who most needs to hear it... and what they should do next. In this prototype, a sample answer is typed out for me.
>
> From those answers the app drafts the whole campaign: a title, a story with sources, ad copy, and who to target. The one thing it never sets is the funding goal. That's always the creator's decision.

## 7. Finish and submit (3:15 – 3:50)

**Do:** On **Basics**, type a name in **Creator or organization name** (for example `Shade Tucson`). Scroll to **Cover image** and tap **Generate with AI**.
- Tap **Continue** through **Story**, **Visuals** (pause a second on the ad previews) and **Reach**.
- On **Submit**, type `2500` as the goal, tick all five statements, and tap **Submit for review**.

**Say:**
> Everything is editable across five steps: basics, story, visuals with live ad previews, reach, and submit. It saves automatically as I go. I add my name, generate a cover, set a goal of twenty-five hundred dollars, confirm the campaign rules, and submit it for review.

## 8. Submitted (3:50 – 4:05)

**Do:** Let the submitted screen settle on the timeline. Tap **Done**.

**Say:**
> Nothing can be backed until it passes review. This timeline follows the campaign from review to its final report. In the prototype, review decides automatically in about twenty seconds.

## 9. Creator dashboard and report (4:05 – 4:35)

**Do:** Tap the **Profile** tab, then **Creator dashboard**. Let the totals count up. Tap the **Completed** tab, tap **Bees Need Weeds: Let Your Lawn Grow**, then tap **Delivery report**. Scroll slowly through **Where the money went** and **Reach**.

**Say:**
> The creator dashboard groups every campaign by stage and always says what to do next. When a campaign finishes, everyone gets a public report: what was raised, the fee, what was spent on ads, what was refunded, and how many people the ads actually reached.

## 10. Activity and close (4:35 – 4:50)

**Do:** Tap the **Activity** tab. The newest item should say your campaign was approved.

**Say:**
> And Activity keeps everyone in the loop: payments, milestones, updates, and, right there, my new campaign has just passed review. That's Campaigns: helping important messages travel further, with proof of what every dollar did.

---

## If something goes off script

- **A step is slower than the script:** keep talking; the next "Say" block starts when its "Do" does, not on the clock.
- **"Your campaign was approved" isn't in Activity yet** (review takes about 20 seconds after submitting): say "and in a few seconds, the review decision lands here" and end there.
- **Sign-in fails:** check the device is online and the sample account exists in Firebase.
- **An error screen appears:** the request failure rate isn't Off. Tap **Try again** and carry on, and set it to Off before the next take.
- **Over time:** cut the Explore sort in scene 2 and the **Est.** sheet in scene 3; that saves about 15 seconds.
